Project Methodology

Core pipeline

Grayscale conversion, noise handling, mean/Gaussian/median filtering, thresholding, morphology, distance transform, marker generation and watershed segmentation.

Why watershed?

Simple thresholding can merge touching cells. Watershed uses marker-controlled regions to split connected objects.

Metrics

MSE measures squared image error. PSNR reports image fidelity in dB. SSIM measures structural similarity.

When a ground-truth mask exists, IoU, Dice, Precision, Recall, F1 and Accuracy are more directly meaningful for segmentation quality.
